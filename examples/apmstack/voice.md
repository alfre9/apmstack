# Voice

How the Northdock PM writes. Part of the bundled sample; every excerpt is invented for the example.

## How I write
- Sentences: short, mostly under fifteen words. One fact each. (from: 6 stories, 2 features, 2026-10-01)
- Temperament: direct and dry. States the problem without softening it. No exclamation marks.
- Opens with: the person and what goes wrong for them. Never with background.
- Words I use: the persona's first name, "kickback", "touchless", "so" (not "in order to").
- Words I never use: "users", "leverage", "seamless", "robust", "simply".
- Formatting: no bold inside sentences. Numbered criteria. Dates as "22 Sep".

## By kind of ticket
- Bugs: what happened, in order. Quotes the error message exactly.
- Stories: context is two sentences at most. Ends the context with the link.
- Features: says what was decided against, and why, in one line each.

## Samples
> Priya opens a kickback and has to message Marcus to find out what happened on the load. He answers the next day. The invoice ages meanwhile.

> We are not building a carrier login. Carriers do not want another portal and Miriam has not cleared it.
