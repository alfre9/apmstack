# Move to per-invoice pricing

- **Decision**: new contracts and renewals are priced per invoice processed, with a platform minimum. (source: pricing committee minutes, 2026-07-14)
- **Decided by**: Tomás Reyes, with Elena Voss and Hannah Lindqvist. See [[stakeholders]].
- **Why**: per-seat pricing fell as customers needed fewer carrier payables staff, which is the outcome we sell. (source: pricing committee minutes, 2026-07-14)
- **Rejected**: staying per seat; pricing on approved spend, which Legal thought looked like a payments fee. (source: pricing committee minutes, 2026-07-14)
- **Consequence**: our margin now depends on touchless rate. See [[metrics]].
