# Personas

Names are the ones the team uses in specs and stories.

## Priya, carrier payables specialist (daily user)
- Works at the brokerage, in finance. Processes 150 to 250 packets a day. (source: onsite visit notes, Halvorsen Logistics, 2026-05-21)
- Job: pay every carrier the right amount, to the right remit-to, on time.
- Today: works the queue in Northdock, with the TMS open in a second window. Keeps her own spreadsheet of exceptions she is waiting on someone else for. (source: onsite visit notes, Halvorsen Logistics, 2026-05-21)
- Measured on invoices processed per day and on payment errors. (source: customer interview, AP manager, 2026-06-11)
- Cannot resolve an exception that depends on what was agreed with the carrier; she has to ask the carrier rep. (source: customer interview, AP manager, 2026-06-11)

## Marcus, carrier rep (occasional user)
- Works at the brokerage, in ops. Books carriers onto loads and owns the carrier relationship.
- Job: cover loads at a good margin and keep good carriers willing to take the next one.
- Today: lives in the TMS, on the phone and in text messages with drivers and dispatchers. (source: onsite visit notes, Halvorsen Logistics, 2026-05-21)
- Measured on loads covered and margin per load. Nothing he is measured on mentions invoices. (source: customer interview, ops director, 2026-06-18)
- 12% of carrier reps with a Northdock seat logged in during the last 30 days. (source: usage analytics by role, 2026-10-20)

## Dana, controller (buyer)
- Runs finance at the brokerage. Signs the contract and owns the renewal.
- Job: close the month on time, keep carrier cost accurate, avoid overpayment, and do it without adding headcount.
- Today: sees Northdock through a monthly summary and through whatever her AP manager escalates. (source: closed-won analysis, 2026-08-20)
- Bought on the promise of fewer carrier payables staff per thousand invoices. (source: closed-won analysis, 2026-08-20)

## The carrier (never logs in)
- An owner-operator or small fleet, often paid through a factoring company.
- Wants to know when they will be paid and why an invoice was short-paid.
- Today: emails or phones the brokerage to ask. (source: CS monthly report, 2026-10-31)
