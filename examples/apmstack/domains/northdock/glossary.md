# Glossary

Industry terms in the sense this team uses them, then internal jargon. (source for all entries unless noted: onboarding wiki "Freight in 30 minutes", 2026-05-12)

## Freight terms
- **Load**: one brokered shipment. Everything else attaches to a load.
- **TMS**: transportation management system, the broker's system of record for loads.
- **Linehaul**: the base rate for moving the load, before any extras.
- **Rate con** (rate confirmation): the agreement between broker and carrier for one load: lane, linehaul, and any accessorials agreed up front.
- **Accessorial**: any charge beyond linehaul. The common ones are detention, lumper, layover and TONU.
- **Detention**: an hourly charge for a driver kept waiting at a shipper or receiver beyond free time, usually two hours. Needs in and out times to prove.
- **Lumper**: a fee paid by the driver to a third party for unloading at a warehouse, reimbursed against a lumper receipt.
- **Layover**: a charge when a driver is held overnight.
- **TONU** (truck ordered, not used): a fee when a load is cancelled after the truck was dispatched.
- **BOL** (bill of lading): the shipping document issued at pickup.
- **POD** (proof of delivery): the BOL signed by the receiver. A **clean POD** has a signature, a date, and no damage or shortage notes.
- **Factoring company** (factor): a finance company that buys a carrier's invoices. Payment must then go to the factor, not the carrier.
- **NOA** (notice of assignment): the letter telling the broker to pay the factor.
- **Remit-to**: the party and account a payment must go to. Getting it wrong means paying twice.
- **Quick pay**: paying a carrier in days instead of net 30, for a fee.
- **Short pay**: paying less than the invoiced amount, with a stated reason.

## Internal jargon
- **Packet**: everything a carrier sends to get paid for one load: invoice, POD, receipts.
- **Touchless**: approved with no human touch. A touch is any human edit, approval click or comment before approval. (source: metrics definition doc, 2026-08-01)
- **Exception**: an invoice Northdock will not approve on its own. Each has a reason code and an owner.
- **The queue**: the exception queue in Northdock, worked by carrier payables.
- **Tolerance**: the difference between invoice and rate con that a customer lets Northdock approve automatically, in dollars or percent.
- **Aging**: how long an exception has been open, in business days.
- **AP**: on this team always means carrier payables, never vendor accounts payable.
- **Ops**: the brokerage's carrier reps and their managers, as opposed to finance.
- **Kickback**: an exception sent from carrier payables to a carrier rep for an answer. Not a payment term here. (source: Workflow squad glossary, 2026-10-22)
