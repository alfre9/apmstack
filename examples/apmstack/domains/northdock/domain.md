# Northdock

> Northdock is a fictional company, bundled with APMstack as a sample domain pack. Every source cited in this pack is invented for the example.

## Product
- Northdock audits and approves carrier invoices for freight brokerages. A carrier sends a packet (invoice, POD, receipts); Northdock reads it, matches it to the load in the broker's TMS, checks it against the rate con, and either approves it touchless or raises an exception for a person to resolve. (source: product one-pager v4, 2026-06-02)
- Northdock approves invoices; it does not move money. The broker pays from its own TMS or bank. (source: MSA template s.3, 2026-01-15)

## Who pays, who uses
- Buyer: the brokerage's controller or VP Finance. See [[personas]]. (source: closed-won analysis, 2026-08-20)
- Daily users: carrier payables specialists. (source: usage analytics by role, 2026-10-20)
- Occasional users: carrier reps, pulled in when an exception needs someone who knows what happened on the load. (source: usage analytics by role, 2026-10-20)
- Carriers and their factoring companies never log in. They experience Northdock only as how fast and how correctly they get paid. (source: product one-pager v4, 2026-06-02)

## Business model
- Annual SaaS contract, priced per invoice processed, with a platform minimum. See [[2026-07-14-per-invoice-pricing]]. (source: pricing decision, 2026-07-14)
- 140 customers, brokerages of 50 to 500 staff. ARR $14.2M. (source: board deck Q3, 2026-10-08)
- Net revenue retention 104%. (source: board deck Q3, 2026-10-08)
- Logo churn 9% annualised. (source: finance churn report, 2026-03-31)

## Stage
- Series B, about 85 people, two product squads (Capture and Workflow). (source: all-hands, 2026-09-04)

## Strategy and current bets, H2 2026
1. Raise touchless rate to 75%. Per-invoice pricing only carries good margin, for us and for the customer, when most invoices need nobody. (source: H2 strategy memo, 2026-07-14)
2. Move upmarket to brokerages above $500M annual freight spend. (source: H2 strategy memo, 2026-07-14)
3. Quick pay add-on deferred to 2027 pending legal review. See [[2026-09-18-defer-quick-pay]]. (source: decision record, 2026-09-18)
