# Constraints

## Regulatory
- Northdock does not move money. Any feature that holds or routes funds raises money-transmitter licensing questions. (source: GC memo on quick pay, 2026-09-10)
- SOC 2 Type II is in force; changes to who can see invoice documents need a control review. (source: security policy, 2026-02-03)

## Technical
- Three certified TMS integrations: LoadPilot, Meridian TMS and FreightDesk. (source: integrations page, 2026-08-12)
- Northdock can write back to the load record only in LoadPilot. Meridian TMS and FreightDesk are read-only. (source: integrations page, 2026-08-12)
- LoadPilot covers 41% of customers. (source: integrations usage report, 2026-08-12)
- The document model is retrained on a six-week cycle; a new field takes at least one cycle to ship. (source: Capture squad runbook, 2026-04-30)

## Contractual
- Financial documents are retained for seven years. (source: MSA template s.9, 2026-01-15)
- Uptime SLA is 99.5%. (source: MSA template s.6, 2026-01-15)
- Eleven enterprise contracts promise that an exception is routed to an owner within four business hours. (source: contracts register, 2026-07-30)

## Organisational
- Two product squads, one designer, no data analyst; the PM pulls their own numbers. (source: all-hands, 2026-09-04)
- Quarterly planning locks on 2026-12-08. (source: planning calendar, 2026-10-01)
- Customers may only be contacted through Customer Success. See [[stakeholders]]. (source: CS engagement policy, 2026-03-17)
